🔁 Official Time to Life Clock Converter: Complete Guide

2024-09-27 — Life Clock

Have you ever needed to know what time "12:00 noon" official is in Life Clock? Or vice versa — what official time does Life Clock 9:00 correspond to? The Official Time to Life Clock Converter performs this conversion instantly and offline.

Online converter

To use the converter, go to the converter section of the site. There you will find two boxes:

  • Right box (Life Clock): with a sunrise color theme
  • Left box (official time): with a monochrome theme

Simply enter a number in either box — the opposite one updates instantly.

Conversion formula

Conversion between official time and Life Clock follows this formula:

Life Clock = official time + (9 hours − local sunrise time)

For example, if today's sunrise in Tehran is at 5:08 official time:

  • Difference: 9:00 − 5:08 = 3 hours and 52 minutes
  • At 12:00 official, Life Clock reads: 12:00 + 3:52 = 15:52
  • At 9:00 Life Clock, official time is: 9:00 − 3:52 = 5:08 (i.e., sunrise)

Why is conversion important?

Converting between official time and Life Clock is useful in several cases:

  1. Daily planning: when you want to know what "9 AM" is in Life Clock.
  2. Alignment with the biological rhythm: to align activities with the body's natural rhythm.
  3. Fasting: to determine the exact time of sunrise and sunset.
  4. Travel across time zones: to understand "where in the natural day" you are at your destination.

Conversion in different cities

The difference between official time and Life Clock varies by city, since sunrise times differ:

  • Tehran (longitude 51.4°): difference of about 3:52 hours
  • Mashhad (longitude 59.6°): difference of about 3:44 hours (earlier sunrise)
  • Tabriz (longitude 46.3°): difference of about 4:20 hours (later sunrise)
  • Shiraz (longitude 52.6°): difference of about 3:51 hours

To convert for your own city, select your city in the widget.

Converter features

Unlike other time converters that only convert between time zones, the Life Clock converter:

  • Calculates based on the real sunrise in your city (not the time zone)
  • Updates automatically each day as the sunrise time changes
  • Works fully offline
  • Has ±2 minute accuracy (with the NOAA formula)
