🌙 Life Clock and Fasting: Suhoor and Iftar Times

2024-09-27 — Life Clock

Fasting is one of the most important applications of Life Clock. During Ramadan, the times of Suhoor (Fajr) and Iftar (Maghrib) are critical. Life Clock helps you naturally understand these times.

Suhoor and Iftar times in Life Clock

In Life Clock, fasting times are displayed as follows:

  • Life Clock 9:00 = sunrise (end of Suhoor)
  • Life Clock 18:00 ≈ sunset (Iftar)
  • Life Clock 6:00 ≈ Fajr (start of Suhoor)

This means Suhoor is about 3 hours before sunrise (Life Clock 6:00) and Iftar is at sunset (around Life Clock 18:00).

Calculating the Suhoor time

The Suhoor time equals Fajr — the moment when the sun reaches 17.7 degrees below the horizon. In the Shia method:

  • Summer: Suhoor around 4:30 AM official time (Life Clock 6:30)
  • Winter: Suhoor around 5:45 AM official time (Life Clock 6:45)

Calculating the Iftar time

The Iftar time equals Maghrib — the moment the upper edge of the sun disappears below the horizon. In the Shia method, a few extra minutes are typically waited to ensure the sun has fully set.

  • Summer: Iftar around 20:00 official time (Life Clock 23:00)
  • Winter: Iftar around 17:10 official time (Life Clock 21:10)

Fasting length in different cities

The fasting length (interval from Suhoor to Iftar) varies across Iranian cities:

  • Tehran: 14–16 hours (depending on the season)
  • Mashhad: 14–16 hours (similar to Tehran)
  • Shiraz: 14–16 hours
  • Tabriz: 14–16 hours

To see the exact time for your city, open the Life Clock widget.

Benefits of using Life Clock during fasting

  1. Natural sense of time: instead of calculating, glancing at Life Clock tells you how much of the fast has elapsed
  2. Alignment with the body's rhythm: align activities with the natural clock
  3. Meal planning: eat Suhoor and Iftar at the right times
  4. Dhikr and prayer: specific times (such as Life Clock 12:00 = 3 hours after sunrise) for dhikr

Golden hours of fasting

In Life Clock, the key fasting times are:

  • 6:00 Life Clock: start of Suhoor (Fajr)
  • 9:00 Life Clock: end of Suhoor (sunrise)
  • 12:00 Life Clock: mid-fast (3 hours after sunrise)
  • 15:00 Life Clock: 6 hours after sunrise (Dhuhr)
  • 18:00 Life Clock: Iftar (sunset)

Tips for fasting with Life Clock

Tip: During Ramadan, use Life Clock as a "second view" alongside official time. This helps you:
  • Know how many hours remain until Iftar
  • Align daily activities with the natural rhythm
  • Take the last hours of the fast (after Life Clock 15:00) a bit easier
