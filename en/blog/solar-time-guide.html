☀️ Tehran Solar Time: Online Calculation of Sunrise and Sunset

2024-09-27 — Life Clock

Tehran solar time is calculated based on the actual position of the sun in the sky — in contrast to official time, which is a regional convention. In this article we examine the calculation method, the difference from official time, and conversion to Life Clock.

What is solar time?

Solar time is based on the most real definition of time: when the sun is at the highest point in the sky (due south for the Northern Hemisphere), solar time is 12:00 noon. This moment is called "apparent noon."

Before and after apparent noon, solar time is computed as follows: every 15 degrees of Earth's rotation equals 1 hour. So when the sun has moved 7.5 degrees past the highest point, solar time is 12:30.

Difference between solar time and official time in Tehran

Iran is in a single time zone (UTC+3:30). However, the mean solar time of Tehran (longitude 51.4° east) is about 3 minutes and 26 seconds behind official time:

Tehran solar time = UTC + 51.4/15 = UTC + 3:25:36
Iran official time = UTC + 3:30
Difference: 4 minutes and 24 seconds

This means when the official clock reads 12:00 noon, the sun in Tehran is actually at its highest point around 12:04.

Solar time in different Iranian cities

The difference between solar time and official time varies across Iranian cities:

  • Tehran (longitude 51.4°): 4 minutes behind
  • Mashhad (longitude 59.6°): 27 minutes ahead (earlier sunrise)
  • Tabriz (longitude 46.3°): 28 minutes behind (later sunrise)
  • Shiraz (longitude 52.6°): 1 minute behind
  • Ahvaz (longitude 48.7°): 13 minutes behind

Equation of Time

Even at a fixed location, solar time changes slightly throughout the year. This happens for two reasons:

  1. Earth's axial tilt (23.5 degrees): the sun appears at different elevations in different seasons.
  2. Earth's elliptical orbit: Earth's orbital speed varies.

This phenomenon is called the "Equation of Time" and causes apparent noon to vary by up to ±16 minutes over the year.

Solar time vs Life Clock

Life Clock is a different concept from solar time:

  • Solar time: 12:00 = sun at the highest point
  • Life Clock: 9:00 = sunrise

Life Clock is more suitable for everyday use, because sunrise is an observable event (unlike apparent noon).

Applications of solar time

  • Prayer time calculation: all prayer times are computed based on solar time
  • Building design: for orientation and shading
  • Agriculture: to determine planting and harvesting times
  • Photography: for golden and blue hours
