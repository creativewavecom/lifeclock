🌅 Tehran Sunrise and Sunset: Exact Time Today

2024-09-27 — Life Clock

The time of Tehran sunrise and sunset is computed every day based on the city's geographic position (latitude 35.69° north, longitude 51.39° east) and the date. In this article we examine the calculation method, seasonal changes, and conversion to Life Clock.

View today's sunrise and sunset online

To see today's exact sunrise and sunset times for Tehran:

  1. Go to the Life Clock widget
  2. Select Tehran as the city
  3. The sunrise and sunset times appear in the "Additional info" section

The calculations use the NOAA formula with an accuracy of ±2 minutes.

Seasonal changes of Tehran sunrise and sunset

The time of sunrise and sunset in Tehran varies significantly throughout the year:

  • Summer solstice (22 June): sunrise 05:08, sunset 20:03, day length 14 hours 55 minutes
  • Winter solstice (1 Dey): sunrise 07:10, sunset 17:02, day length 9 hours 52 minutes
  • Spring equinox (20 March): sunrise 06:15, sunset 18:30
  • Autumn equinox (22 September): sunrise 06:00, sunset 18:10

Calculation method

Sunrise and sunset times are computed based on three main parameters:

  1. Latitude: determines the angle at which the sun rises
  2. Longitude: determines the UTC hour at which the sun rises
  3. Date: determines the sun's elevation (declination)

The main formula:

cos(H) = (cos(Z) - sin(lat) × sin(decl)) / (cos(lat) × cos(decl))

Sunrise hour = 12 - H/15 - Equation of Time
Sunset hour = 12 + H/15 - Equation of Time

Where Z = zenith angle (90.83° for official sunrise/sunset), and H = the sun's hour angle.

Day length in Tehran

The day length (the interval between sunrise and sunset) in Tehran varies throughout the year:

  • Longest day (June): 14 hours and 55 minutes
  • Shortest day (Dey): 9 hours and 52 minutes
  • Difference: 5 hours and 3 minutes

These changes are caused by Earth's axial tilt (23.5 degrees).

Sunrise and sunset in Life Clock

In Life Clock, sunrise is always 9:00 and sunset is around 18:00 (depending on day length). This means:

  • Life Clock 9:00 = sunrise
  • Life Clock 18:00 ≈ sunset
  • Life Clock 21:00 = 3 hours after sunset (start of the evening wind-down)

Sunrise and sunset in other cities

Sunrise and sunset times differ across Iranian cities:

  • Mashhad: sunrise 6 minutes earlier than Tehran
  • Tabriz: sunrise 28 minutes later than Tehran
  • Shiraz: sunrise 1 minute later
  • Bandar Abbas: sunrise 9 minutes earlier

Photography golden hours

For photographers, the golden hour times matter:

  • Sunrise to 1 hour after: soft warm light
  • 1 hour before sunset to sunset: warm golden light
  • Blue hour: 20–30 minutes after sunset
