🕌 Tehran Prayer Times: Online and Offline Calculation

2024-09-27 — Life Clock

Tehran prayer times consist of the five main prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) that are computed every day based on Tehran's geographic position (latitude 35.69° north, longitude 51.39° east). In this article we examine the calculation method, accuracy, and how it relates to Life Clock.

How Tehran prayer times are calculated

Prayer times are calculated based on the position of the sun relative to the horizon. For Tehran, we use the Shia (Jafari) method:

1. Fajr

Fajr begins when the sun reaches 17.7 degrees below the horizon. At this moment, the dawn light (white light) appears on the eastern horizon. For Tehran in mid-summer, Fajr is around 4:30 AM official time, and in winter around 5:45 AM.

2. Sunrise

Sunrise is the moment when the upper edge of the sun appears on the eastern horizon. This is computed with a zenith angle of 90.83 degrees (including atmospheric refraction). In Life Clock, sunrise is always 9:00.

3. Dhuhr

Dhuhr is when the sun reaches the highest point of its daily arc (transit across the meridian). In Tehran, this varies between roughly 12:10 and 12:30 official time. In the Shia method, 1–2 minutes are typically added for precaution.

4. Asr

Asr is the moment when the shadow of a vertical object equals its own height (plus the noon shadow). This is called the "1× shadow" method and is common in the Shia school. For Tehran, Asr is usually 3–4 hours after noon.

5. Maghrib

Maghrib corresponds to sunset (the disappearance of the upper edge of the sun below the western horizon). In the Shia method, a few extra minutes are typically waited to ensure the sun has fully set.

6. Isha

Isha is when the eastern redness (sunset's red light) disappears and the sun reaches 14 degrees below the horizon. In summer this is about 1–1.5 hours after Maghrib, and in winter about 1 hour after Maghrib.

Accuracy of the calculations

Our calculations use the NOAA formula (from the U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration):

  • Sunrise and sunset: ±2 minutes
  • Dhuhr: ±1 minute
  • Fajr and Isha: ±5 minutes

The reason Fajr and Isha are less accurate is that at low angles (17–18 degrees below the horizon), atmospheric refraction is more variable and depends on atmospheric conditions (temperature, pressure, humidity).

The relationship between prayer times and Life Clock

Life Clock is defined by the real sunrise, so it has a natural connection to prayer times:

  • Life Clock 9:00 = sunrise
  • Life Clock 12:00 ≈ 3 hours after sunrise
  • Life Clock 18:00 ≈ sunset

This means that in Life Clock, prayer times are displayed relatively. For example, Dhuhr is always around 15:00 Life Clock (6 hours after sunrise).

Why do prayer times change every day?

The position of the sun in the sky changes throughout the year for two reasons:

  1. Earth's axial tilt (23.5 degrees): causes the sun to appear at different elevations in different seasons.
  2. Earth's elliptical orbit: Earth revolves around the sun at a variable speed.

As a result, sunrise and sunset in Tehran vary from 5:08 (summer) to 7:10 (winter).
